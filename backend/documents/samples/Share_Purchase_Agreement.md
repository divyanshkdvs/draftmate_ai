# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**THIS SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT** is entered into on 12th April 2026, by and between:
1. **The Promoters / Sellers** specified in Schedule 1;
2. **Horizon Global Growth Fund LP** ("Purchaser"); and
3. **OmniRetail Networks Pvt. Ltd.** ("Target Company").

### 1. PURCHASE AND SALE OF SHARES
Subject to the terms herein, Sellers agree to sell and transfer to Purchaser, and Purchaser agrees to purchase from Sellers, 1,250,000 Equity Shares at a price of INR 480 per share.

### 2. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT
Closing is conditional upon obtaining statutory clearances from the Competition Commission of India and satisfaction of representations under the Companies Act, 2013.

### 3. INDEMNITY
Sellers shall indemnify Purchaser against tax claims and undisclosed liabilities subject to an aggregate cap of 20% of the Purchase Price.
