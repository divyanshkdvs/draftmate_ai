# SENIOR EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

**THIS EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT** is made on 1st February 2026, by and between:
1. **Apex Digital Innovations Pvt. Ltd.** ("Employer"); and
2. **Mr. Vikramaditya Singhania** ("Executive").

### 1. DUTIES AND APPOINTMENT
Executive is engaged as Chief Technology Officer and shall devote full professional working hours and fiduciary diligence to the Employer.

### 2. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
Gross CTC shall be INR 60,00,000/- per annum. Executive is eligible for statutory gratuity under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 and ESOP allocations under the 2026 Incentive Scheme.

### 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY WORK FOR HIRE
All code, models, and architectures authored during tenure shall vest exclusively with Employer under Section 17 of the Copyright Act, 1957.

### 4. GOVERNING LAW
Governed by the laws of India with exclusive jurisdiction in the Courts of Bengaluru, Karnataka.
